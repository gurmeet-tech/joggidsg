— FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about my services, process and delivery.

The process
What types of videos do you edit?

Short-form for TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts, long-form YouTube and podcast cuts, plus motion graphics, captions and thumbnails. If it lives on a screen, I can probably cut it.

What do you need to get started?

Raw footage or a link, your goal for the video, any title or hook direction, and a reference or two for pacing and style. That's it — I'll handle the rest.

How do revisions work?

Every project includes two rounds of revisions. We review the cut together, then I tighten pacing, captions and sound until it is ready to publish.

Delivery & revisions
How fast is the turnaround?

Short-form edits usually land in 24–48 hours. Longer videos and motion-heavy projects depend on scope, but you'll always get a clear deadline before we start.

How do revisions work?

Every project includes two rounds of revisions. Timestamped feedback in one message keeps things fast — larger scope changes are quoted separately before any extra work.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

Yes. Monthly slots are limited and prioritised for creators posting weekly. You get consistent turnaround, a shared project space and priority scheduling.
